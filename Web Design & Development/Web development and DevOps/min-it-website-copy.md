## FAQs

### Solutions FAQ

**1. Can we run more than one lending model on the same platform?**
Yes. Lenders commonly run SACC and MACC alongside consumer or asset finance in one system,
each with its own product rules, documents, fees and workflows. Origination, servicing,
payments and reporting stay in one place, so your team isn't moving between systems as your
product range grows.

**2. How does Min-IT handle NCCP and responsible lending obligations?**
Compliance is built into the workflow. Protected Earnings Amount assessment is enforced at
approval for SACC lending, serviceability and affordability checks run as part of the
application, hardship assessment and default handling follow NCCP-compliant workflows. Every
decision is recorded in an audit trail, and 60+ NCCP and ASIC-aligned reports are exportable
on demand, including investor reports.

**3. How do you handle KYC, PEP and AUSTRAC screening?**
Identity verification and screening run inside origination through GBG GreenID and Equifax
ID Matrix, covering PEP and sanctions checks aligned to AML/CTF obligations. Credit and
affordability data comes from Equifax, TaleFin and CreditSense, including open-banking
serviceability. Results attach to the application, so the evidence is on file if you're ever
asked for it.

**4. Which payment rails does Min-IT support?**
Direct debit and credit across BECS and the NPP through Zepto, and card payments through
GlobalPay. Instalment scheduling, dishonour handling, disbursements — including splits to
multiple payees for asset finance — reconciliation and payout tracking are managed in the
Payment Portal.

### Pricing FAQ

**1. How does Min-IT pricing work?**
There are two parts to it. A flat monthly platform access fee covers the entire platform
(Min-IT CRM, Min-IT LMS, Min-IT Pay and the Member Portal), and a usage fee is charged per
contract, so your cost moves with your book. There's no separate line item for compliance
workflows, because they're part of the platform rather than an add-on.

**2. Do we pay per module, or for the whole platform?**
The whole platform. Origination, servicing, payments and the borrower Member Portal are
included in the one platform access fee. You're not quoted a base system and then charged
again for the parts that make it usable, and you don't need to decide up front which modules
you'll grow into.

**3. What counts as "usage"?**
Usage is charged per contract, so it tracks the loans you actually write and service. Quiet
months cost less than busy ones. This is the part of the model that stops a smaller lender
paying for enterprise scale they haven't reached, and it means your costs rise in step with
your book rather than ahead of it.

**4. Are the integrations included?**
Yes. Equifax, TaleFin, CreditSense, GBG greenID, Equifax IDMatrix, Zepto (NPP and BECS) and
GlobalPay are part of the platform, and we don't charge a build fee to switch one on. What
each provider charges you for their own data, verification or transaction services sits under
your commercial agreement with them.

### Products FAQ

**1. Where is our data hosted?**
Our data is hosted in Australia. Min-IT runs on Microsoft Azure in the Australian region,
with separate sandbox and production environments, so borrower and credit data stays onshore.
Handling of that data follows the Privacy Act 1988, the Australian Privacy Principles and the
Credit Reporting Privacy Code.

**2. Do we have to use every module?**
Every module is included, so this is a question about rollout rather than licensing. Lenders
often start with origination and servicing, bring payments across once that settles, and open
the Member Portal when their team is ready for it. Nothing has to be bought again later.

**3. Do we get a sandbox environment to test in?**
Yes. Every lender gets a sandbox environment alongside production, both hosted in Australia.
New products, credit rules, fee structures and contract templates are configured and tested
there before they reach a live borrower.

**4. Can Min-IT connect to the systems we already run?**
Yes. There is an open API and webhooks for broker and aggregator portals, B2C front ends and
whatever else sits in your stack. Credit and open banking data comes through TaleFin,
CreditSense and Equifax. Identity, PEP and AUSTRAC-aligned screening runs through GBG greenID
and Equifax IDMatrix. If something you use is not on that list, tell us what it is and we
will tell you whether it is a configuration job or a build.
