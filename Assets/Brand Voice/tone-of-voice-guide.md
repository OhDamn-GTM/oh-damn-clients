# CashLab — Tone of Voice Guide

_Derived from `Demand Capture/AEO-SEO/content-strategy.md`. Source of truth for brand facts, personas, and compliance detail remains that file; this guide governs how we sound, not what we claim._

---

## 1. Who We Are (the short version)

CashLab (legal entity Villashore Pty Ltd, trading as CashLab / Cash Lab) is a regulated Western Australian small-loan lender, with stores in Midland and Geraldton, WA, plus a pawnbroking side business. Licensed as a WA lender, accepts online applications Australia-wide. Products are SACC ($300 to $2,000) and MACC ($2,001 to $5,000). Client contact: Adam.

**Core stance:** honesty over marketing. The core content differentiator is correcting competitor oversell ("guaranteed approval," "instant $10,000") rather than out-marketing it.

## 2. Voice Dials

| Dial | Setting | What that means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Warm + factual | Not corporate, not salesy, not clinical |
| Shame framing | None | Borrowing, including on Centrelink income, is treated as normal |
| Harm reduction | First, always | Free alternatives (NILS, Centrelink advance, hardship arrangements, financial counselling) are presented before any CashLab product pitch |
| Honesty | Above marketing | Core differentiator is correcting competitor oversell, not outselling it |
| Spelling/English | American English by default | Australian spelling retained for genuinely local terms/institutions |
| Case | Sentence case | Not title case |

## 3. Target Personas

Use these to anchor angle and trust signals in every piece of content.

| Persona | Who they are | Triggered by | What builds trust |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The Regional Working Aussie | Full-time/casual worker in WA, fair-to-good credit, no savings buffer | Something broke, payday is 8 to 14 days away | Local presence, plain-English fees, ACL/ASIC visibility |
| 2. The Emergency Solver | Any WA adult with an unexpected bill (car, vet, dental, medical) | Bill just landed, needs money in 24 to 72 hours | Fast, clearly stated turnaround; reassurance on credit history |
| 3. The Life Transition Aussie | Facing a planned but expensive event (moving, bond, school costs) | Predictable expense, savings won't stretch | Empathetic tone, no shame framing, clear repayment maths |
| 4. The Cautious Comparer | Skeptical, doing homework before applying | Vetting the lender before/instead of applying | ACL number, ASIC registration, honest regulatory content |

Persona 4 is CashLab's real differentiator: almost no competitor writes deliberately for the skeptical, research-first borrower.

---

## 4. Banned / Excluded

Never use, mention, or imply, in any channel:

- Any competitor name, anywhere, under any framing (Cash Train, Cash Converters, Nimble, Sunshine Loans, Money3, City Finance, or any other lender) — except where a keyword itself requires naming one (e.g. "Cash Converters alternative" content), and even then, the surrounding copy never disparages the competitor
- Crypto / investing, in addition to debt consolidation and gambling (products/purposes CashLab excludes)
- "Guaranteed approval," "instant $10,000," or any claim implying approval before assessment
- Unverifiable claims (e.g. a "cash boost" or government program that can't be confirmed active) — exclude rather than guess

---

## 5. Mechanical Rules

- No em dashes.
- Sentence case, not title case.
- American English by default; Australian spelling retained for genuinely local terms/institutions.
- Never let an AI image generator render embedded facts, numbers, or regulatory text into graphics — generate images with minimal/no text, add real data as a separate, proofread overlay.
- URL slugs: always the short, deliberately chosen slug from the brief, never auto-generated from the title.
- Meta descriptions: count characters programmatically (155 to 160 target), don't eyeball it.

---

## 6. Compliance-Critical Facts (never vary these without checking)

- SACC: $300 to $2,000, 16 days to 12 months, fees capped at 20% establishment + 4%/month.
- MACC: $2,001 to $5,000, 16 days to 24 months, fees capped at $400 establishment + 48% p.a.
- SACC debt-spiral cap: total repayment (including all fees) can never exceed double the original loan amount.
- 90-day bank statement check is a legal requirement for every SACC/MACC lender, not CashLab-specific.
- CashLab accepts all income types equally: full-time, part-time, casual, self-employed, Centrelink.
- 10% protected earnings rule applies when more than 50% of a borrower's income is from Centrelink; exact basis (gross vs net income) is unconfirmed, verify against ASIC RG281 before repeating.

The mandated National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 disclosure text (Facts Library, Section 10 of the content-strategy file) must appear verbatim, unvaried, on every published post that discusses borrowing costs.

---

## 7. Honesty Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- Never name a competitor, in any framing, except where the keyword itself requires it (and never disparage even then).
- Never present a "Centrelink loan," "$500 loan," or similar as a distinct product with a special limit; CashLab has no separate product for these, only its standard SACC/MACC range.
- Never guess at unverifiable claims (e.g. government programs, jurisdiction of law) rather than confirming them.
- Harm-reduction options come before any product pitch, especially for financially vulnerable personas (see Persona 4, and the "Unemployed loan Australia" content angle).

---

## 8. Quick Self-Check Before Sending/Publishing Copy

1. Does this sound warm and factual, or does it slip into salesy/clinical?
2. Any competitor named without the keyword requiring it, or named with any negative framing?
3. Any em dashes, title case, or unverified claims?
4. Does every compliance fact match Section 6, and does the mandated disclosure text appear verbatim where required?
5. Are free/harm-reduction options presented before the CashLab pitch, where the persona calls for it?
6. Could this line have been written about any competitor? If yes, make it more specific to CashLab.
