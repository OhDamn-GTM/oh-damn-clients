# TBA Firefly - Strategy & Active Initiatives

## Programme structure
Delivered via Bound's phased RevOps framework. Two slightly different phase namings appear across documents, both describe the same work:

| Phase (MAP) | Phase (SOW) | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quick Wins | Foundations & Audit | 1-2 | Full audit, CRM blueprint, contact cleanup, pipeline rebuild |
| GTM Build | CRM & Data Foundations | 3-8 | Segmentation, sales workspace, lifecycle alignment, workflow rebuild |
| Optimise | Sales Enablement & Operational Rollout | 9-12 | Activity automation, 3CX integration, ticketing, website integration |
| Embed & Scale | Visibility, Attribution & Scale | 13-16+ | Dashboards, attribution, CPD visibility, marketing foundations, group rollout |
